Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Jalebi Jams is a personal music experience created by Omkar. This notice explains what information may be processed when you visit the site and how optional Google Analytics is used to help improve it.

Google Tag Manager and analytics

Jalebi Jams loads a Google Tag Manager container when you visit the site. The container ID is GTM-B837K9PK. Google Tag Manager is used to manage website tags. Analytics collection depends on which tags are configured in that container; if Google Analytics 4 is configured, it may process information about your visit, such as pages viewed, interactions with the site, browser and device details, and identifiers stored in cookies. Google may process IP addresses to provide and protect its services; handling can depend on your region and product settings.

Analytics is intended to help understand general site usage and improve Jalebi Jams. We do not intentionally configure analytics events to send song titles, audio, contact details, or other information you enter into the site. Tags in the Google Tag Manager container may use cookies or similar technologies depending on their configuration. For details, see Google's information about Analytics data, Google Tag Manager, and Google's Privacy Policy.

This version of Jalebi Jams does not display an analytics accept/reject or cookie settings control. If you do not want analytics, you can use browser controls to block or clear cookies and other site data; this may not prevent all tag requests. Review the Google privacy links above for Google's available controls.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. When your browser requests pages and files, GitHub may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to retrieve those fonts. These services process information under their own policies: GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

Audio and personal information

Jalebi Jams does not ask you to create an account. The player processes audio in your browser. This site does not intentionally collect or transmit your music files, selected tracks, or contact details to Google Analytics.

Your choices and contact

You can reject analytics or change your choice from Cookie settings in the homepage footer. Questions about this notice can be sent to omkangane07@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current site setup and is not legal advice. It may be updated if the site's services or data practices change.
